Glossary

One definition per term, so every team — Store, CRM, Analytics, leadership — means the same thing by the same word.

Foundation
Account 360
One live, unified profile per customer, joining Store, CRM, Books, Desk, Projects, Payments, Support and Marketing. Already built — the platform starts here.
Business 360
The next altitude up: not just what we know about the customer, but about their business — product, financial, and operational maturity across Zoho.
External Intelligence
Context from outside Zoho — company website, industry data, public signals, enrichment APIs. Sourced from trusted providers, not indiscriminate scraping.
Signal / Event
A single timestamped thing that happened in a source system — payment failed, trial started, license reduced. The raw material everything is built from.
Feature Store
The prepared, reusable inputs (derived from signals) that scores and agents read — computed once, used everywhere.
Scores
Shared Score
A 0–100 number computed once and read by every agent, so no two agents disagree on the same fact. An opinion, not a verdict.
Health Score
Is the account thriving or slipping? The master gauge that gates expansion and raises retention priority.
Churn Score
How likely the account is to leave. High churn blocks cross-sell and triggers retention before renewal.
Expansion Score
How ready the account is to buy more — only high for healthy, growing accounts.
Payment Risk Score
How likely the next payment is to fail. Drives the Recovery Agent.
Fraud Score
How likely a transaction or account is fraudulent. Separates a billing problem from a genuine threat.
Agents
Specialist Agent
A narrow expert that looks at one slice and recommends one action. It never decides on its own — it reports to the NBA Engine.
Recommendation
An agent's structured proposal: the action, its success probability, the ARR at stake, and the cost to act.
Recovery Agent
Recovers failed payments — retry, card-update, alternative method. The first agent to build (cleanest attribution).
Cancellation Rescue Agent
Intervenes at cancellation with the right save for the stated reason — pause, downgrade, monthly plan.
Bundle Economics Agent
Detects when a customer's standalone stack is pricier than Zoho One (or vice versa) and surfaces the cost comparison.
Renewal / Expansion / Trial Agents
Further specialists for on-time renewal, cross-sell to healthy accounts, and trial-to-paid conversion.
Decision layer — the brain
NBA (Next Business Action) Engine
The layer that decides. It ranks every agent recommendation by expected value and lets one action through. The platform's real intelligence.
Expected Value
The single comparable number each recommendation is reduced to: (probability × ARR at stake) − cost. Replaces hardcoded priorities.
Policy Engine
Hard business rules that can remove any action regardless of its value — max discount, one save offer per year, no cross-sell to at-risk accounts.
Capacity Engine
Treats human time as finite. If 10,000 accounts need attention and there are 50 AM slots, it picks the best 50.
Communication Budget
Caps how often a customer is contacted — one well-chosen touch instead of five emails, an SMS, and a WhatsApp at once.
Execution & learning
Execution Channel
Where an action goes out — Email, WhatsApp, SMS, in-product, AM task, partner task, wallet offer.
Surface
How the same decision is shown to a given audience — Cliq alert, Mail digest, Analytics dashboard, CRM card.
Outcome Store
The system's memory — records what happened after each action (sent, viewed, accepted, renewed, churned). Without it, there is no learning.
Holdout
A small control group that gets no intervention, so the platform can prove an action caused a result rather than rode along with it.
Learning Loop
The feedback path that retunes scores and probabilities from real outcomes, so recommendations improve over time.
Lift
The measured difference between treated accounts and the holdout — the platform's true, causal impact on ARR.
