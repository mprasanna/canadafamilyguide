Section 01 · The problem

The missing layer between Zoho's data and Zoho's action.

Zoho already holds more customer intelligence than any competitor. The gap is not data — it is a decision layer that reads all of it and answers one question: what should happen next, for this account, right now?

6+
Systems holding the data
Store, CRM, Books, Desk, Projects, Payments — each with its own view. No single place assembles the picture and decides.
0
Systems that decide
Dashboards describe what happened. Reports surface trends. Nothing recommends a specific action for a specific account at the moment it matters.
∞
Accounts needing a response
Failed payments, at-risk renewals, usage drops, ticket spikes — all happening simultaneously across every account. No team can cover all of them manually.
The question this platform answers
"Given everything Zoho knows about this customer — their payments, their usage, their tickets, their renewal — what is the single best action to take right now, and who should take it?"
Section 02 · The platform

Eight layers. One governed action.

The platform is not a collection of automations. It is a closed loop — signals become scores, scores wake agents, agents compete, the NBA Engine decides, guardrails protect, one action executes, and the outcome sharpens the next decision.

What just happened in plain language: Zoho knew ABC Corp's payment failed, their usage was declining, and renewal was approaching. Instead of three separate emails from three separate systems, the platform made one decision — fix the payment first, hold the rest — and delivered it through the right channel to the right person. That is decision intelligence.
