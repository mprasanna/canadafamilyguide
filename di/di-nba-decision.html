The NBA (Next Business Action) Engine

Agents don't act. They each recommend. The NBA Engine is the layer that decides — ranking every recommendation by expected value, removing anything Policy forbids, and letting exactly one action through.

Worked through one account — ABC Corp

2NBA computes expected value & ranks

No hardcoded priorities. The engine turns each recommendation into one comparable number and sorts.

Expected Value = ( probability of success × ARR at stake ) − action costthe single number every recommendation is reduced to

Why Recovery wins over Cancellation — both target the same $24,000, so they're competing routes to the same save, not separate gains. The engine takes the cheaper, higher-probability route first. The monthly-plan offer isn't discarded; it's held as the fallback if recovery fails. Renewal is contingent — a reminder is pointless until the payment is fixed, so it's queued behind recovery.

3Policy Engine gates the ranking

Expected value proposes; Policy disposes. Hard business rules can remove any action, no matter how high it ranks.

PASSRetry payment — no discount, no money-moving concession. Within all limits. Cleared to run.
BLOCKBundle / Zoho One cross-sell — Policy forbids expansion offers to at-risk accounts (open payment failure + churn 78). Removed regardless of its expected value.
PASSMonthly-plan fallback — within the discount cap and the "one save offer per account per year" limit. Allowed, but only if recovery fails.
↓
4Capacity & Communication Budget allocate

The action survived. Now: does it warrant a scarce human slot, and how many times may we touch this customer?

SLOTHuman capacity — at $24,000 with 85% recoverability, ABC Corp ranks inside today's top AM slots. A task is created. A $300 account would not have qualified.
1 of 1Communication budget — this is the single allowed touch this cycle. The renewal reminder and any cross-sell mail are suppressed so they don't pile on.

5One action is released
Next Business Action — ABC Corp
Retry payment + send card-update link
Routed to the AM as a task, raised in Cliq, folded into the morning Mail digest, and shown with its reasoning on the CRM card. One decision, rendered for each audience. The outcome — recovered or not — is written to the Outcome Store, which sharpens the next decision.
The whole point in one line: the agents supply judgment, but the NBA (Next Business Action) Engine — with Policy and Capacity above it — is what turns four competing opinions into a single, defensible, business-safe action. That arbitration layer is the platform's real intelligence.
