05
NBA EngineDecides
Ranks every recommendation by expected value (probability × value − cost) and picks the best action. No hardcoded priorities.

06

Hard business rules always win — e.g. max discount 20%, one save offer per year, no cross-sell when churn score > 80.

07

Protects people and customers. If 10,000 accounts need attention and there are 50 AM slots, it picks the best 50 — and sends one well-chosen touch, not five.
