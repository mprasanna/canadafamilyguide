Engineering log

Not a plan any more.
A record.

One entry per week, newest first. What was attempted, what broke, what the evidence said, what changed as a result. The plan pages describe intent; this page is the only one that can be wrong about the past.

Rule for this page

Entries get written the week they happen, including the weeks that went badly. A log with no failures in it is not a log — it is marketing, and any engineer reading it can tell.

What happened, in one line

Two or three sentences of plain prose. What you set out to do, what actually occurred, and the number that came out of it. Numbers are what make an entry worth reading a year later — odometry error 4.1% over 10 m, down from 9.6% after re-measuring the effective wheel radius beats improved odometry every time.

If something broke: the symptom, the hypothesis you tested, what the bag actually showed, the root cause, and the change. If the hypothesis was wrong, say so — that is the most credible thing on the page.

Updating the counters

The week, phase and criteria figures in the strip at the top of every page come from one config block near the start of each file. Change the numbers there and all four pages agree. The tick bar draws itself: one tick per week, filled to the current week, taller ticks at the six phase gates.

window.PROGRESS = {
  week: 7,  phase: 2,  phaseName: 'Drivetrain & firmware',
  criteria: 12,  repo: 'https://github.com/you/robot-architect'
};

Setting repo once activates every code, ADR and evidence link across all pages — they are dashed and inert until it is filled in.

03 — What this becomes

The progression is the argument.

Anyone can claim a finished robot. What is hard to fake, and hard to dismiss, is forty-four dated entries showing the machine accreting, the numbers improving, and the wrong hypotheses recorded next to the right ones.

The risk of a public notebook

A page that says Week 1 of 44 for six months is a visible stall, and more damaging than no page at all. This only pays if the counters move — which is the same reason it is worth having.
