Layer 03 · Intelligence Processing

How PIP thinks

Two distinct compute types — and the line between them is never crossed. This separation is what makes the platform governable, auditable, and trustworthy.

Deterministic Engine
Catalyst · Weighted · Replayable
Same inputs always produce the same outputs. No inference, no randomness.
PIP Health Score (0–100) — weighted composite of Commercial Stability (30%), Operational Reliability (25%), Renewal Continuity (25%), Escalation Impact (20%)
Zoho Partner Score (0–1000) — official tier-determining model: Commercial Health, Customer Success, Market Readiness, Zoho Engagement
Trajectory — weighted slope of 12-month health history. Recent months weighted 1.4×. Outputs: label, velocity (pts/month), acceleration flag
Trajectory Urgency — combines health score + direction + velocity into a single sort key. A partner at 72 declining fast may outrank one at 48 stabilising
Risk Classification — threshold crossing, SLA breach detection, renewal proximity alerts
Benchmark Position — cohort comparison from Analytics daily sync. Anonymous. Recomputed quarterly
Zia · AI Interpretation
Narrative · Synthesis · Read-only
Receives computed scores as structured input. Produces plain-language operational intelligence. Cannot alter any score or decision.
Intelligence Summary — one headline encoding state + trajectory: "TruePartner is declining — executive review required"
Operational Summary — 2–3 sentence causal prose explaining why the score is where it is, with historical pattern matching
Signal Interpretation — 5-dimension synthesis (Operational Reliability, Trajectory, Revenue Concentration, Capability Depth, Coordination)
Coaching Recommendations — ranked actions from weakest scoring dimensions, each with estimated ARR or Partner Score impact
Benchmark Language — contextual peer comparison in plain English: "CRR top 15% among Premium partners in Americas"
Zia does NOT: compute scores · fire alerts · route escalations · write to audit log · alter sort order
If Zia is unavailable — scores, trajectory, and all alerts continue normally.
Key boundary
The deterministic engine produces the operational truth. Zia explains it in human language. A PAM can always trace any Zia-generated sentence back to the specific score inputs that caused it — because Zia only receives computed values, never raw signals. This is what makes the interpretation auditable.
Trajectory states
↑ Accelerating
Improving rapidly
Weighted slope >2.5 pts/month. Velocity itself increasing. Pattern aligns with partners approaching Tier-1 contribution.
↑ Recovering
Improving from a low
Weighted slope 0.5–2.5 pts/month. Previously declining or flat. Monitor for 2 more periods before reducing attention.
→ Stable
Holding position
Slope within ±0.5 pts/month. No significant directional movement. Monitoring posture.
↓ Decelerating
Slowing down
Weighted slope −0.5 to −2.5 pts/month. Velocity slowing — deteriorating forces partially offset. Watch, not emergency.
↓ Declining
Actively deteriorating
Weighted slope <−2.5 pts/month with acceleration. Urgency score elevated. May outrank a lower-scoring partner who is stabilising.
Urgency model
Not just score
trajectoryUrgency() = health + direction + velocity. A partner at 72 declining fast ranks above one at 48 stabilising. Direction matters more than state.

Layer 05 · Full Picture

The intelligence lifecycle

Signal → Intelligence → Prioritisation → Action → Outcome. Every artifact in this documentation covers one stage of this lifecycle.

Stage 01
Signal
182 events / 24h · 8 source systems
Operational events from CRM, Desk, Billing, Renewals, Certifications, Store, Campaigns, and Analytics are ingested by Catalyst. Every signal is timestamped and attributed to a source system. Nothing is inferred at this stage — these are raw facts.
Signal Detail →
Stage 02
Intelligence
Deterministic engine + Zia interpretation
Catalyst computes PIP Health Score, Zoho Partner Score, trajectory velocity, urgency rank, and benchmark position — deterministically. Zia receives the computed values and generates plain-language summaries, signal interpretations, and coaching recommendations. The two compute types never mix.
Technical Ref →
Stage 03
Prioritisation
trajectoryUrgency() · sort order · attention allocation
The portfolio is sorted by trajectory urgency — not score alone. A partner at 72 and declining fast ranks above one at 48 and stabilising. The PAM sees the partners who need attention now, not the ones who score lowest on a snapshot. Direction matters more than state.
Open Platform →
Stage 04
Action
CRM · Store · Cliq · Mail
Intelligence surfaces in three delivery channels. CRM delivers Partner 360, PAM Command, and Director views to internal teams. Store delivers the Partner Console — progress-framed, scoring redacted — to the partner principal. Alerts fire through Cliq (escalation channels) and Mail (weekly digests, threshold breaches).
Open Platform →
Stage 05
Outcome
Escalation prevention · renewal stabilisation · expansion
Partner deterioration is caught before the renewal is lost. Certification decay is flagged 45 days before it reduces the Partner Score. Expansion-ready customers are surfaced before the window closes. Every PAM action and system decision is logged immutably — creating a closed governance record of what was known, when, and what was done.
