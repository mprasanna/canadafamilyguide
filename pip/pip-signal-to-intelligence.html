From Signals to Intelligence

How PIP's 182 daily signals move through the Deterministic Engine and into Zia-powered narrative interpretation — and where the boundary between the two sits.

Where the 182 signals come from

Every number in PIP traces to a specific event in a source system. The 182 is a 24-hour rolling count across 8 systems — not a static figure. Each signal is a timestamped fact: a ticket opened, a cert expiring, an invoice aging past 45 days.

What the Deterministic Engine computes

Catalyst ingests the signals and runs them through a weighted scoring model. The output is always the same given the same inputs — no randomness, no model inference at this stage. PAMs can adjust weights via the Tweaks panel; every adjustment is logged.

Trajectory — a weighted slope of the 12-month health score history. Input is the PIP Health Score time-series computed above — not raw signals directly. How Zia interprets the scores

Zia · AI
Zia is Zoho's AI layer — the natural fit for PIP's interpretation stage given that PIP lives inside the Zoho ecosystem. Zia receives the computed scores as structured input and produces plain-language operational summaries. It does not have access to raw signals or write access to any score field.

Once the Deterministic Engine produces its output, Zia receives a structured context payload per partner. It generates four types of text:

Output type What Zia generates Where it appears
Intelligence Summary One headline sentence encoding state + trajectory: "TruePartner is declining — executive review required" Partner 360 header
Operational Summary 2–3 sentence prose explaining the causal pattern behind the current score. References historical pattern matches where applicable. Partner 360 · PAM summary strip
Signal Interpretation 5-dimension synthesis (Operational Reliability, Trajectory, Revenue Concentration, Capability Depth, Coordination) — each 1–2 sentences. Partner 360 interpretation grid
Coaching recommendations Ranked actions derived from the partner's weakest scoring dimensions. Each includes an estimated ARR or Partner Score impact. Partner 360 · Partner Console

An example of the translation from score to language:

Deterministic Engine output → Zia input
Health: 41 · Trajectory: Declining · Velocity: −3.2 pts/mo · CRR: 84.1 · Escalations open: 7 · Certs expiring: 6 · ARR at risk: $1.78M (42%)
"TruePartner has destabilized. Renewal continuity, escalation discipline, and certification depth are deteriorating in concert. This pattern matches partners that lost one or more enterprise renewals within six months in 64% of historical cases. Top-3 customers carry 42% of ARR exposure. Executive review recommended this week."

The boundary between the two

This distinction is what makes PIP auditable. Governance requires that the deterministic layer is never touched by inference.

Deterministic Engine

  • Computes Health Score (0–100)
  • Computes Partner Score (0–1000)
  • Classifies trajectory + velocity
  • Fires threshold alerts via Catalyst
  • Routes escalations by RBAC
  • Writes to the immutable audit log
  • Drives portfolio sort order

Zia · AI Interpretation

  • Writes narrative summaries
  • Synthesises signal patterns into prose
  • Generates coaching recommendations
  • Produces benchmark comparison language
  • Cannot compute or alter any score
  • Cannot fire alerts or route escalations
  • Cannot write to the audit log
  • Cannot change portfolio sort order

If Zia is unavailable or returns an error, every score, trajectory, and threshold alert continues to function normally. The deterministic layer has no dependency on Zia.
