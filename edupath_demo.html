05 / what crosses, what doesn't

Same architecture. Same observations. Two scopes.

The four mockups above are powered by one shared brain. The privacy boundary between Sage and Atlas isn't a policy — it's enforced at the filesystem level. Here is what each surface sees of the same underlying memory.

Sage sees

  • Verbatim journal entries
  • Named friends, teachers, teammates
  • Real-time emotional content
  • Mood, stress, social context
  • Private notes she redacts at will
  • The conversation history, fully
  • Her own trajectory file in plain markdown
Privacy Boundary

Atlas sees

  • Aggregated thematic patterns
  • Direction vectors and door states
  • Confidence values and freshness tiers
  • Course selections, grades, contests
  • Themes — never the words behind them
  • "Interpersonal strain" — never the name
  • The freshness footer on every output

The Tuesday FRC chat above lives entirely on the left side. What crosses to the right side is the pattern, with no name, no quote, no escalation.
