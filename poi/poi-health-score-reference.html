Health Score · Calculation Reference

Exact sub-score weights, signal normalisation curves, and worked examples. Every number in the POI Health Score traces to a specific formula on this page.

Master formula · POI Health Score (0–100)
Health Score  =
  Commercial Stability  ×  0.30
  + Operational Reliability  ×  0.25
  + Renewal Continuity  ×  0.25
  + Escalation Impact  ×  0.20
Each sub-score is 0–100 · maximum Health Score = 100 · weights are PAM-adjustable via the Tweaks panel · every adjustment is logged in the immutable audit trail

Sub-score 01

30% weight Commercial Stability Zoho Books · CRM · Zoho Store

Three signal sources are blended into a single 0–100 sub-score. ARR growth carries the most weight because it is the most direct indicator of commercial momentum.

SignalSourceWeight within sub-scoreHow it scores
ARR Growth (YoY %)
Year-over-year revenue growth rate
 CRM 50% Normalised against a 0–100 curve. See table below.
Invoice Health
Aging invoices + payment retry count
 Zoho Books 30% 100 − (aged invoices × 12) − (retries × 5). Floor: 0.
Renewal Revenue Health
CRR % + auto-renew rate from Store
 Zoho Store 20% CRR % maps directly to score (96.6% → 96.6). Auto-renew rate blended in.

ARR Growth normalisation curve

Growth % is normalised to a 0–100 score against regional baseline. Negative growth never scores above 35. Flat growth (0–5%) sits in the 35–55 zone. The highlighted row is the $1,000 → $1,300 example (30% growth).

Example · ARR $1,000 → $1,300 (30% growth)
ARR growth sub-score  = 89  (from curve above)
Invoice health        = 75  (1 aged invoice · no retries: 100 − 12 − 0 − 13 buffer = 75)
Renewal health       = 80  (CRR 91% + moderate auto-renew rate)
Commercial Stability  = (89 × 0.50) + (75 × 0.30) + (80 × 0.20)
                       = 44.5 + 22.5 + 16.0
                       = 83
This 83 then contributes: 83 × 0.30 = 24.9 pts to the overall Health Score.

Sub-score 02

25% weight Operational Reliability Zoho Desk · Catalyst

Starts at 100 and deductions are applied based on ticket volume stress and escalation severity. A partner with zero open escalations and normal ticket volume scores close to 100.

SignalSourceWeight within sub-scoreHow it scores
Ticket Volume Stress
Rolling 7-day count vs 30-day baseline
 Desk 40% Normal volume = 0 deduction. Spike >130% baseline = −10 to −20.
SLA Breach Status
Active SLA timer breaches
 Desk · Catalyst 40% Each breach deducts. See deduction table below.
CSAT Average
Trailing customer satisfaction score
 Desk 20% CSAT 5.0 = no deduction. Each 0.5 below 4.0 = −5 pts.

Sub-score 03

25% weight Renewal Continuity Zoho Store · CRM

Primarily driven by CRR. A partner with a CRR of 96.6% scores approximately 96.6 on this dimension — the mapping is near-direct. Review-stage overdue renewals apply a secondary deduction.

SignalSourceWeight within sub-scoreHow it scores
CRR (Customer Retention Rate)
Renewed ÷ up for renewal × 100
 Zoho Store 60% CRR % maps near-directly. CRR 96.6 → score contribution 96.6.
Auto-renew Rate
% of renewals completed without manual intervention
 Zoho Store 25% Auto-renew rate >80% = strong. Below 60% = significant deduction.
Review-stage Overdue
Renewals past expected decision date
 Zoho Store · CRM 15% Each overdue review = −4 pts. Aged >14 days = −8 pts per renewal.
Example · CRR 91%, auto-renew 72%, 2 overdue reviews
CRR contribution:      91.0 × 0.60 = 54.6
Auto-renew contribution: 72 maps to ~76 score × 0.25 = 19.0
Review-stage deduction: −4 × 2 overdue = −8 × 0.15 = −1.2
Renewal Continuity = 54.6 + 19.0 − 1.2 = 72.4

Sub-score 04

20% weight Escalation Impact Zoho Desk · Catalyst

Starts at 100. Each open escalation deducts points based on severity. SLA breaches carry a higher penalty than non-breach escalations. The score floors at 0 — it cannot go negative.

Escalation typeDeduction per open escalationNotes
Sev-1 with active SLA breach−18 ptsHighest severity. Resolving removes the deduction immediately on next cycle.
Sev-1 without SLA breach−12 ptsSerious but within SLA window.
Sev-2 with active SLA breach−14 ptsSLA breach elevates Sev-2 significantly.
Sev-2 without SLA breach−6 ptsStandard deduction for open Sev-2.
Floor0Score cannot go below 0 regardless of escalation count.
Example · 7 open escalations (3 Sev-1 breach + 2 Sev-1 + 2 Sev-2)
Start: 100
− 3 Sev-1 breach: 3 × −18 = −54 → 46
− 2 Sev-1 no breach: 2 × −12 = −24 → 22
− 2 Sev-2 no breach: 2 × −6 = −12 → 10
Escalation Impact = 10 (contributing 10 × 0.20 = 2.0 pts to Health Score)
