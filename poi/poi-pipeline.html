Catalyst ingests, normalises, and weights all raw signals
— Catalyst Engine Deterministic computation. Fully auditable. No AI.
Deterministic computation layer
Normalises all raw signals, applies governance weights, and computes every context score. Recomputation is triggered on weight changes and is audit-logged. Scoring functions are deterministic — the same inputs always produce the same outputs. No AI involvement at this layer.

Interpretation and synthesis layer
Explains what patterns mean operationally. Converts raw telemetry into operational significance. Cross-synthesises signals from renewals, tickets, certifications, and trajectory into a single operational narrative.
Assistive · interpretive · non-authoritative
Zia does NOT
Never touches the deterministic layer. Zia does not compute scores, fire escalations, mutate health values, decide Sev-1 routing, alter governance thresholds, or directly change urgency. Those remain deterministic and auditable. This is why the platform feels trustworthy rather than AI-hyped.
