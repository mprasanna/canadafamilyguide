04
Finance
Budget Engine
How each partner's MDF allocation is calculated and tracked. Every figure is live — no manual spreadsheets.
Budget Cap
Set once per fiscal year based on prior-year commissionable revenue
=
Revenue × Tier %
Tier rates (configurable)
Authorized 30% · Advanced 40% · Premium 20%
Committed Amount
Sum of approved amounts where status is PAM Approved / Approved / Executed / Payout Pending
=
Σ approved requests
Paid Amount
Sum of disbursed payouts from mdf_payouts where status = paid
=
Σ paid payouts
Available Balance
Recalculated on every status change
=
Cap − Committed − Paid
Over-balance rule
If a request exceeds Available Balance, a warning shows on the partner's form and on the PAM's review screen. PAM must actively acknowledge before approving. Request is not blocked at submission.
Zero-balance rule
If Available Balance = $0, the partner cannot submit. They see: "Your MDF balance is fully committed. Contact your PAM." PAM or Admin can manually override the cap.
Payout calculation
Payout = min(actual spend × co-fund %, approved cap, available balance). Ensures the partner can never receive more than what was approved or what remains.
